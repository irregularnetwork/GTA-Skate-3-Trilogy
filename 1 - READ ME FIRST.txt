===============================================================================
                              VICE CITY SKATE
          GTA Vice City + Skate 3's skateboarding, in one game
===============================================================================

 QUICK START
 -----------
  1. Right-click the "Vice City Skate" zip > Extract All... > extract it to
     a simple folder like  C:\Games\Vice City Skate
  2. Open that folder and double-click  "2 - Play Vice City Skate"
  3. Answer the two questions (your GTA Vice City folder, and your
     Skate 3 default.xex - you can drag them into the window).
  4. In the game, wait ~15 seconds, then press J to skate.

  Need help finding those two things? Read on.

 You never need to open the "Files" folder.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Walk up anywhere in Vice City, press J, and Tommy drops onto a skateboard
driven by Skate 3's own physics, flick-it tricks, grinds and bails, while the
city carries on around you: people react, cops chase you, cars run you over.

This download contains NO game files. You connect your own copy of
GTA Vice City and your own copy of Skate 3 during setup (steps below).
Your original game folders are only read, never changed.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU NEED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [ ] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
 [ ] GTA Vice City for PC - the ORIGINAL 2002 version
       (Steam, Rockstar Games Launcher, or the old CD version all work.
        The "Definitive Edition" does NOT work.)
 [ ] Skate 3 for Xbox 360, extracted to a folder (see step 2)
 [ ] An Xbox controller (or any XInput controller) - needed to skate
 [ ] About 2 GB of free disk space


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 1 - FIND YOUR GTA VICE CITY FOLDER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Setup usually finds it by itself. If it doesn't, here's where it is:

 * Steam: in your Library, right-click "Grand Theft Auto: Vice City"
   > Manage > Browse local files. That window is the folder.
   (Usually ...\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto Vice City)

 * Rockstar Games Launcher: usually
   C:\Program Files\Rockstar Games\Grand Theft Auto Vice City

 * CD version: usually
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Rockstar Games\Grand Theft Auto Vice City

The right folder has these inside: "models", "data", "anim", "audio" and
the file gta-vc.exe.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 2 - GET SKATE 3's default.xex (from your own copy)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Setup needs the file "default.xex" from Skate 3 for Xbox 360, with the
game's "data" folder next to it, like this:

      Skate 3\
        default.xex
        data\
          big\ ...
          content\ ...

How to get that folder from your own copy:

 * From a disc image (.iso) of your disc: download extract-xiso
   (https://github.com/XboxDev/extract-xiso), then run
       extract-xiso -x "Skate 3.iso"
   It creates the folder with default.xex and data inside.

 * From a digital (Games on Demand) copy on your Xbox 360 hard drive:
   copy the game's package to your PC and open it with a tool such as
   Velocity, then extract everything to a folder.

An .iso file by itself does NOT work - it has to be extracted first.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 3 - INSTALL AND PLAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Extract the zip (right-click > Extract All...) to a simple folder that
    you can write to, for example:  C:\Games\Vice City Skate
    Not inside Program Files, and no accents or symbols in the path.
    Tip: before extracting, right-click the zip > Properties > tick
    "Unblock" > OK. Windows then won't warn about every file.

 2. Double-click  "2 - Play Vice City Skate"
    (If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and
     then "Run anyway".)

 3. A window asks two questions:

      Step 1 of 2: your GTA Vice City folder
        - If it shows "Found: ...", just press Enter.
        - Otherwise drag your Vice City folder into the window
          (or paste its path) and press Enter.

      Step 2 of 2: your Skate 3 default.xex
        - Drag the default.xex file (or the folder it's in) into the
          window and press Enter.

 4. Wait a minute while it copies Vice City and converts the Skate 3 data.
    Then the game starts by itself.

 From then on, "2 - Play Vice City Skate" starts the game straight away.

 In the game: stand still for about 15 seconds after loading (Skate 3 loads
 in the background), then press J.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 CONTROLS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 On foot
   J  or  click both sticks in ............ get on the board

 Skating (controller, Skate 3's flick-it controls)
   A or X .................................. push
   Left stick .............................. steer / lean
   Right stick: pull down, then flick up ... ollie
   Right stick, other flicks ............... flip tricks
   Triggers ................................ grabs
   Other buttons ........................... as in Skate 3
   J  or  click both sticks in ............ get off (keeps some of your speed)

 Keyboard while skating
   1 ....................................... speed push
   2 ....................................... big jump

 Skate tools menu:  Ctrl+M  (then open "VC Skate")
   Speed push / big jump amounts, gravity (moon jumps), push strength,
   ollie height, no bails, show grind rails (the cyan lines), show skate
   info. Arrow keys to move, Enter or Left/Right to change, Ctrl+M to close.
   Your choices are saved.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 "Vice City Skate is not unzipped yet"
     You opened it inside the zip. Extract the zip first (Quick Start, 1).

 "That folder is not GTA Vice City"
     Pick the folder that has models, data and anim inside (see step 1).
     The Definitive Edition doesn't work.

 "That is not a default.xex file" / "data folder ... is incomplete"
     Point it at the extracted Skate 3 folder from step 2, with the data
     folder beside default.xex. An .iso doesn't work.

 "This folder is read-only" / "move the Vice City Skate folder"
     Move the whole Vice City Skate folder to e.g. C:\Games and run
     "2 - Play Vice City Skate" again.

 "Windows protected your PC"
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (Or unblock the zip before
     extracting, see step 3.)

 J says "Skate 3 is still loading"
     Wait 15 seconds after the game loads and try again.

 I can get on the board but can't move
     Plug in an Xbox controller - skating uses the controller.

 The game crashes when I Alt+Tab or click away while it starts
     That's reVC's fullscreen mode. Start it again, or play windowed:
     open Files\game\reVC.ini, and under [VideoMode] set Windowed=1.

 Start setup over
     Delete the folder Files\game and run "2 - Play Vice City Skate" again.

 Turn skate mode off
     In Files\game\reVC.ini under [VCSkate], set Enabled=0.

 Something else went wrong
     Look at Files\game\skate.log - it says what happened.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 KNOWN ISSUES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 * Parked cars aren't solid for the board (you ride through them). Moving
   cars knock you off.
 * You can't get in a vehicle while skating - get off first.
 * Skating ends by itself when you die, get busted, a cutscene or mission
   takes control, you're teleported, the game saves or loads, or you land
   in water.
 * "No bails" stops most bails, but not every single one.

 UNINSTALL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Delete the "Vice City Skate" folder. Your original GTA Vice City and
